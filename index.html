About me

Support experience from both sides of the ticket

I started in e-commerce customer support in 2021, answering email and chat for an online automotive supplies store and later a print-on-demand brand. That meant order tracking, returns, refunds, design revisions and escalations, usually several cases at once and always by the SOP.

While supporting customers, I completed a BS in Computer Science at the University of Nueva Caceres, graduating Cum Laude and on the Dean's List every year. From August 2025 to September 2026 I built AI business applications at Noah Business Applications: a CRM, a supplier quote system, HR automation and document tools. I also documented, tested, deployed and demoed them to stakeholders.

That combination is what I bring. I understand support workflows from the agent's chair and from the system's side. When something breaks, I gather the details, reproduce the problem, find the cause and write it up clearly, the same way I'd handle a customer case.

How I work

  • Accuracy first. I check order details, data and figures before anything goes out.
  • Follow the process, flag the gaps. I work to your SOPs and point out where they could be clearer.
  • Document as I go. Notes, guides and diagrams so the work stays readable after me.
  • Keep people updated. Clear, professional communication with customers and teammates.
  • Keep learning. New help desks, project tools and AI-assisted workflows are part of the job.

Procurement Automation

QUOT: AI Quote Comparison

Turns a pile of vendor quotes (PDFs, Excel sheets, CSVs) into a side-by-side comparison and a recommended award.

The need

My role
Developer: built the system end to end, from quote reading and scoring to the supplier portal.
Features & technical details

Personal Finance Web App (PWA)

Budget App

A mobile-first, installable budgeting app with an AI assistant that answers questions like "Can I afford this?" and "Where did my money go?" using your real numbers.

The need
Track spending, bills and installments in one private place, without trusting the server with raw financial data.
My role
Sole developer: design, backend, frontend, security, testing and deployment.
Features & technical details
  • Daily spending allowance, multiple accounts, recurring bills and installments, subscriptions, savings goals, a calendar heatmap, charts, and CSV import/export.
